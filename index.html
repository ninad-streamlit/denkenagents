What we build for you

Most agent projects deliver a clever demo that nobody ends up owning. We deliver three things together, because any one of them alone is what makes the other two fail.

Agents built around your work

We start from how your business actually runs — who decides what, where the judgement sits, what repeats every week. The agents map onto roles you already recognise, and each is built to written requirements and accepted on criteria you can check yourself.

The governance layer, built in

Identity, mandate, spending limit and audit trail go in with the agent, never bolted on afterwards. Every action traces to exactly one name, every permission is revocable by you, and every euro is attributed before it is spent.

Day-to-day operation

We run them. Agents wake on schedule, are monitored for silence as well as for errors, escalate what they cannot decide, and are repaired, re-budgeted and improved as your business changes — with a named human who answers when something goes wrong.

How we work

You approve the roster before anything is built. Most agent projects fail at the first step rather than the last: somebody automates a task nobody had described.

Understand the work

We map what your team does all day — the repetition, the judgement calls, the handovers and the places where things quietly go wrong.

Design the roster

We propose the agents by role, each with duties, limits and a reporting line. You approve that roster — and what each one may never do — before a line is written.

Build to spec

Each agent is built against written requirements, tested, and accepted on criteria agreed with you. Unproven work is not finished work.

Govern from day one

Identity, policy, spending limits and the record go in with the agent, not after it is already running in your business.

Operate and improve

Monitoring, cost control, escalation handling and upgrades — with a monthly account of what the agents did and what they cost.

Governed, managed, custom built

Three words that are easy to claim. Here is what each one has to mean before we will put an agent into your business.

The claim behind the three words: an agent that is merely capable is a liability. What makes it safe to run is boring and unglamorous — a name, a mandate, a limit, a record and a person at the end of it. No single one of those produces accountability; they only work together, and removing one weakens the rest.

Every floor under the same rules — which is what makes the whole building answerable.
Custom built

Built around your work, not a template

The roster comes out of your processes. An agent nobody in your business recognises the job of is an agent nobody will trust with it.

Traceability

Every action carries one name

Named agents on their own accounts — never a shared login. "Who did this?" always has exactly one answer, enforced by the system rather than by convention.

Governed money

Budgets that refuse before they spend

Every agent carries a limit checked before the work, not reported after it. Ungoverned spend is worse than undeclared spend.

Managed

Silence is treated as a fault

An agent that stopped working looks identical to one with nothing to do. We monitor for both, so quiet failure does not sit undiscovered for a month.

Evidence

Claims check against records

A check that could not run is not a pass. We would rather tell you something is unproven than show you a green tick that means nothing.

Human mapping

Every agent ends at a person

Each agent maps to someone in your organisation who carries the accountability for it. Escalation has one ruled route, and the last rung is always a human.

Who it is for

Three conversations we have most often.

We drown in the same work every week

Repetitive judgement work a person still has to touch — intake, triage, chasing, reconciling, reporting. If the work is describable, it is buildable.

We can't deploy what we can't explain

Regulated or client-facing work where a black box is not an option. Here governance and audit are the product, not the paperwork around it.

We tried a chatbot and nobody owned it

A pilot that answered questions, drifted, and quietly became nobody's job. We start by deciding who is accountable and build backwards from there.

We want our own agent team, not a licence

The agents run on subscriptions you own, against your data, under rules you can change. You are not renting access to somebody else's black box.

We do not just build them — we run one

Carolverse is our own estate: dozens of named AI agents with roles, records, budgets and a constitution, building, governing, auditing and narrating their own work every day, answerable to one human. It is where every rule on this page was learned — including the ones that were learned the hard way.

It runs in public. You can read the encyclopedia of it, follow the daily work log, and see what it cost, before you ever talk to us about yours. We would rather you judged us on a system you can inspect than on a case study you cannot.
